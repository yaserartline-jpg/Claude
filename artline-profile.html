About

Artline Engineering is a construction and fit-out company based in Fujairah. Our team of experienced professionals delivers interiors for retail, food and beverage, healthcare, government and residential clients, with an in-house carpentry and furniture workshop and an aluminium and glazing division.

Vision

  • To be recognised as a leading provider of high-quality construction and fit-out services in the UAE.
  • To lead the market by offering superior quality at competitive prices.

Mission

  • To deliver exceptional workmanship that surpasses market standards.
  • To exceed clients' expectations and support our employees' expertise.
