Who we are

Built on detail, trusted across the UAE

Artline Engineering is a construction and fit-out contractor with offices in Dubai and Fujairah. We deliver complete interiors for retail, food and beverage, healthcare, government and residential clients.

Design, joinery, aluminium and glazing, and site works are all handled in-house. That keeps quality, cost and programme in one team's hands, from first drawing to handover.

Vision

To be the UAE's first call for construction and fit-out, known for high-quality work at a fair price.

Mission

To deliver workmanship above market standard on every project, and to keep growing the skills of the people who build it.

How we deliver

  1. 01

    Brief & site survey

    We walk the site with you, confirm the scope, budget and programme, and measure up.

  2. 02

    Design & approvals

    Our technical office develops the design and shop drawings and secures authority and landlord approvals.

  3. 03

    Workshop production

    Joinery, counters and furniture are built in our own carpentry workshop while the site is prepared.

  4. 04

    Site works

    Our site team runs civil, MEP coordination, finishes and installation under one programme.

  5. 05

    Handover & aftercare

    Snagging, completion certificate, and support through the defects liability period.
