About us

Built in Fujairah, trusted across the UAE

Artline Engineering is a construction and fit-out contractor based in Fujairah. We deliver complete interiors for retail, food and beverage, healthcare, government and residential clients.

Design, joinery, aluminium and glazing, and site works are all handled in-house. That keeps quality, cost and programme in one team's hands, from first drawing to handover.

Vision

To be recognised as a leading provider of high-quality construction and fit-out services in the UAE, offering superior quality at competitive prices.

Mission

To exceed clients' expectations with workmanship above market standards, and to keep developing the expertise of our people.

Services

One contractor, from drawing to handover

Building contracting

All kinds of building projects under our Fujairah contracting licence.

Interior fit-out & decor

Turnkey shops, restaurants, clinics and offices in malls and standalone buildings.

Carpentry & furniture

Counters, display units, wall cladding and loose furniture from our own workshop.

Aluminium & glazing

Shopfronts, glass partitions and framed glazing for retail and office spaces.

Design & approvals

Design development, shop drawings and authority approvals by our technical office.

Maintenance & renovation

Re-partitioning, unit merges and refurbishment of occupied commercial spaces.
